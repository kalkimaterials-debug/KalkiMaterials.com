01 — Engineered oak

FB Hout

Engineered European oak, built for coastal interiors.
The material

Oak floor, wall and ceiling cladding — for the Caribbean.

Oak brings warmth and texture to a white house: on the ceiling, the feature wall, the joinery, and underfoot in the bedroom. FB Hout's engineered oak is built for the islands. A European oak wear layer on a cross-laminated, exterior-grade (WBP) birch plywood core keeps it stable in humid, coastal air, and it installs by full-surface glue-down.

In place

Oak as floor, wall, and ceiling

Laying

Patterns

Two classic parquet patterns for rooms that call for more than straight planks, available on request.

Chevron
Laid at 45°. The ends meet in a point, creating a continuous arrow line that runs the length of a room. It works best in corridors and long spaces.
Herringbone
Laid at 90°. Rectangular blocks interlock in a staggered zigzag. It's the traditional parquet pattern: structured and timeless.
