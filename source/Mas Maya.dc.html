Mas Maya

Lime-based plaster system, from floor to pool.
The material

One continuous surface, from floor to pool.

Mas Maya is a limestone plaster, applied by hand in thin layers: soft, matt and slightly cloudy where the light rakes across it. It runs across floors, walls, ceilings, wet rooms, façades and pools without tile lines or grout, so a room reads as formed rather than built. Breathable, flexible and water-resistant, it's made for humid climates, with zero VOC.

In place

Walls, floors, wet rooms, built-in furniture
The application

Made by hand, on site.

Mas Maya is a system applied by hand in thin layers and worked until the texture reads right, which is why no two walls are exactly alike. Kalki supplies the material and the know-how, and if you need one, we can connect you with a trained applicator on the island.

Specification

The build-up

Two build-ups cover every surface. The wall build-up suits smooth interior walls and ceilings. The floor build-up, reinforced with mesh, suits floors, wet areas, façades, tile, wood and furniture.

01
Primer
One coat. Bonds the system to tile, wood, brick, plaster or plasterboard.
02
Base coat with mesh
Floor build-up only, 2 mm. Reinforces the surface.
03
Finish layer
Wall 3 mm, floor 2 mm. Where the texture of the surface is made.
04
Sealing
Optional on walls. On floors and wet areas: presealer and sealer.
Total thickness: wall about 3 mm, floor about 4 mm.
