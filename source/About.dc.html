About

We import the material language you already design in.

Kalki Materials is a curated material distributor for Aruba, Bonaire and Curaçao, built around a problem you will know: the palette you draw is rarely the palette you can get.

The story

It started with a sourcing problem.

Anyone building on these islands knows the routine. The client shows you a reference from the Mediterranean coast. You can draw it and render it, and then you spend weeks trying to find anyone who can deliver the surfaces, in the right finish, on a schedule the contractor will accept. More often than not, the palette gets compromised somewhere between the mood board and the invoice.

We went to the source instead. Four European manufacturers, chosen because their work already speaks to each other: oak that warms a room, a limestone finish that makes walls continuous, a composite that carries the wood look onto the terrace, and porcelain that turns a pool into a feature. Then we built the import side ourselves, with consolidated freight, customs and delivery, so you deal with one relationship instead of four.

We're not a warehouse. We're the curated channel between four European manufacturers and your project.

What we stand on

Three things we don't compromise on.

01
Curation over catalogue

Four lines, not four hundred. Every material we carry has to work alongside the others in one project, or it doesn't belong in the library.

02
Built for this climate

Salt air, UV and humidity are the test. Where a coastal option exists, we specify it, such as 316 stainless steel fixings on the Tarimatec system.

03
Trade only

Kalki works business to business. We supply architects, interior designers and contractors, with specification support and delivery all the way to your project.
