04 — Porcelain

Wow Design

Wellness porcelain for pools and wet areas, with ceramic pieces for architectural features.
The material

Colours made for water, pieces made for architecture.

Wow Design is a Spanish ceramic design studio based in Castellón, on the Mediterranean coast. Its wellness porcelain, Sukabumi, Marble and Aqua, is made for pools, terraces and bathrooms, in white, sand, grey, green, blue and graphite, designed to merge with the water. The surface is matt, R11 / Class 3 non-slip and frost-resistant, for floors and walls, indoors and out.

Beyond the water, Icon and Reef, in matt stoneware, bring the same Mediterranean palette into the room: Icon is designed by and for architecture, and Reef is a block designed to play with light. One material language runs from the pool lining to the shower, the feature wall and the room divider.

Where it goes

Applications
