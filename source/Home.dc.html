Why this exists

One relationship for the whole surface palette.

The palette is the Mediterranean one: the texture of whitewash plaster, the warmth of light oak, natural materials that carry from the living room to the terrace and the pool.

Many of your clients know the feeling of the Mediterranean coast and want it in the Caribbean. Kalki brings four European manufacturers into one relationship, and we handle specification support, import and delivery.

One call. The full surface library for a project, delivered together.

How we work

One supplier between your drawings and four European factories.

Kalki is the single supplier for four specialist European manufacturers on the ABC islands. We chose them because their products hold up in salt air, UV and humidity, and we handle everything from specification to delivery on site.
